The high temperatures and pressures require specialized materials and designs to withstand the conditions, while the acid rain and sulfuric acid droplets pose a significant threat to the spacecraft's electronics and instrumentation. Additionally, the dense atmosphere creates a strong drag effect, which can slow down the spacecraft and make it difficult to maintain a stable orbit or trajectory. The atmosphere also scatters and absorbs radio signals, making communication with Earth a significant challenge. To overcome these challenges, spacecraft must be designed with specialized heat shields, pressure vessels, and communication systems that can withstand the extreme conditions on Venus.
