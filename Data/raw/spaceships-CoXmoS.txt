Q: What is a solar sail, and how does it work in space?

It works by deploying a large, thin reflective material, such as Mylar or aluminium, which is pushed by the sun's photons. As the photons bounce off the sail, they transfer their momentum to the spacecraft, propelling it forward. This innovative technology has the potential to revolutionise space travel, enabling spacecraft to travel farther and faster without the need for traditional fuels. With its lightweight and efficient design, solar sails could be used for a variety of missions, from interplanetary exploration to asteroid deflection.
